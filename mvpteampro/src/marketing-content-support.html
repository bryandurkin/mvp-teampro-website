Frequently Asked Questions

Do you manage social media?

Ongoing social-media management is not our primary service.

We can create or repurpose content that can be distributed through social channels as part of a broader content program, but our focus is the underlying content and customer communication, not spending every day managing social accounts.

Can you work with content we already have?

Absolutely.

In many cases, that is the best place to start.

Videos, presentations, webinars, podcasts, articles, case studies, proposals, and existing customer material can all become useful source material.

Can you write newsletters and email campaigns?

Yes.

We can help create the copy, format the campaign, prepare the audience, schedule it, test it, and manage the recurring execution around it.

Can you repurpose videos and podcasts?

Yes.

Long-form recorded content can often become articles, emails, newsletter material, smaller video assets, sales content, and additional website resources.

Can you publish the content too?

Yes.

Depending on your systems and the scope of the engagement, our team can help move content from draft through formatting and publishing instead of simply handing you another document to deal with.

Do we need a full marketing strategy before starting?

Not necessarily.

If the strategic direction is already clear, we can focus heavily on execution.

If the direction is not clear, we first need enough agreement around the audience, message, objectives, and priorities to avoid producing content just for the sake of producing it.

What’s the difference between this and MVP Authority Hub?

Marketing & Content Support is broader execution support.

MVP Authority Hub is specifically focused on converting company expertise into owned, structured authority content and measuring how that material performs in AI-driven discovery and citations.

Can we use both?

Yes.

For some companies, Authority Hub creates the core expertise and authority assets while MVP Team Pro supports the broader email, customer communication, repurposing, and campaign execution around them.

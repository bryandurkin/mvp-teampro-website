Case Study 1 · B2B Software

625 Decision Makers. 48 Demos. 20 New Clients.

B2B SoftwareSpecialty application-software company

A specialty application-software company needed a consistent way to reach and stay in front of a defined group of decision makers through a long B2B sales cycle.

The challenge was not generating a single campaign. It was maintaining the follow-up long enough for opportunities to develop.

What MVP helped manage:

  • contact research and validation
  • consistent outreach
  • phone follow-up
  • email follow-up
  • direct mail
  • CRM activity
  • appointment generation
  • long-term follow-up
625Decision makers
48Product demonstrations

What made the difference: the campaign did not depend on one email, one salesperson, or one short burst of activity. The audience was worked consistently over approximately 24 months. That persistence created conversations that would have been easy to miss in a traditional campaign.

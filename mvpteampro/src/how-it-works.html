From Fit Call to Monthly Report

Your part: the Fit Call, approving what goes out, and reading the monthly report. Our team does the rest.

  1. Fit Call

    We look at your customer list and what you’ve published, then recommend 5x5 Outreach, Authority Hub, or both.

  2. Set Up

    Our tech lead connects to your CRM, email system and website. Our team builds your list or your topic plan.

  3. Run It

    Callers run the 5x5. Content creators write, design and publish. You stay in control of what goes out.

  4. Hand Off

    When a contact responds, we hand the conversation to your team with the record updated. Content goes live once you approve it.

  5. Monthly Report

    Every month you get a report on the work and the results, so you never have to chase an update.

FAQ

Questions Business Owners Usually Ask

Who does the work?

Trained team members in the Philippines, led by our U.S.-based leadership team. They’re trained on your business and work inside your CRM.

Who manages the team, and what do I have to manage?

Our U.S.-based leadership team manages the work, checks quality, and covers absences. Your part is the Fit Call, approving what goes out, and reading the monthly report.

How do you train the team on our business?

Before launch, your team learns your customers, your offers, and your systems, and we write down the workflow, timing, and expectations. Then we test it before anything goes out.

Do you work inside our existing CRM?

Yes. GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Jobber, ServiceMonster, and most other CRMs. Our tech lead connects to it during setup.

What hours does the team work?

Coverage is set around your customers and your sales cycle. We settle it on the Fit Call.

What happens if a team member is unavailable?

Our leadership team covers absences, so the work keeps moving and you don’t have to find a replacement.

Can we start with a small scope of work?

Yes. Many engagements start with one focused workflow and expand only when it creates clear value.

How soon can we get started?

It depends on your scope and your systems. We set the start date together on the Fit Call.

FAQ

Questions Home Service Owners Usually Ask

Do you answer calls for our business?+

Yes. Depending on the engagement, MVP can provide inbound phone support, scheduling, qualification and other customer-service functions.

Can you follow up estimates that have not closed?+

Yes. Estimate and opportunity follow-up is one of the areas where having a defined process and somebody responsible for executing it can make a significant difference.

Can you contact old customers in our CRM?+

Yes. We can help clean and update customer data, segment past customers and execute appropriate reactivation and reminder campaigns.

Do we have to switch CRMs?+

Usually not. We prefer to work inside the systems you already use whenever they can support the process effectively.

Do you replace our office staff?+

Not necessarily. In many companies we work alongside the existing office, sales or customer-service team and take ownership of work that currently does not have a reliable owner.

Can this work across multiple franchise locations?+

Yes. Repeatable processes are particularly useful for multi-location companies because the same operating framework can be deployed while still allowing for location-level differences.

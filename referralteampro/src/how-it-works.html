Frequently Asked Questions

How involved do I need to be?

We need your expertise and direction, especially early on.

But the goal is to remove as much routine research, follow-up, CRM work, and coordination from your plate as possible.

Do I personally have to handle all the outreach?

Not necessarily. Referral Team Pro can support initial outreach and routine follow-up, then bring you in where your involvement matters most.

Do we need to change CRMs?

Usually not. Whenever practical, we work inside the systems you already use.

Is this automated?

Parts of the process can be supported by automation, but referral development is still human-led.

We use technology to improve consistency and efficiency, not replace genuine relationships.

How long does it take to see traction?

Referral development is relationship development. Some opportunities move quickly. Others take time.

The goal is to build a repeatable system, not force an artificial timeline.

Can this work with our existing marketing?

Yes. Existing articles, case studies, newsletters, videos, events, and customer stories can all help support referral relationships.

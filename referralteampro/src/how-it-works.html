Step by Step

What Actually Happens After We Decide to Work Together

Step 1: Define the Referral Opportunity

Who Should Know You?

Before outreach starts, we define:

  • Your ideal customer
  • The types of people who already influence them
  • Industries and roles worth targeting
  • Geography
  • Service or product fit
  • Potential relationship value
  • Reasons a referral partner might care

This becomes the foundation for the program. The better the target, the more useful the outreach.

Step 2: Research & Validate the Right Partners

Build a Focused Relationship List

Once the target is clear, we research potential referral partners. That can include:

  • Company research
  • Role validation
  • Contact information
  • Geographic fit
  • Industry fit
  • Existing overlap
  • Complementary services
  • Relationship potential

We validate and organize the information before outreach begins. The result is not simply a list of contacts. It is a working referral-development database.

Step 3: Build the Outreach Plan

Give People a Reason to Respond

Referral outreach should not begin with “Can you send us referrals?” That is too early. We develop the messaging around:

  • Who you are
  • Why the relationship makes sense
  • What you have in common
  • How you may be able to help each other
  • What would make the conversation useful

Depending on the audience, the outreach may include phone, email, direct mail, invitations and useful content.

The goal is not maximum volume. The goal is the right conversation.

Step 4: Start Real Conversations

Human Outreach Matters

Our team begins the outreach and documents what happens. That can include:

  • Phone calls
  • Emails
  • Voicemail
  • Direct mail
  • Meeting invitations
  • Follow-up
  • CRM updates

When someone responds, the next step depends on the relationship. That might mean:

  • Scheduling a meeting
  • Continuing the conversation
  • Sending useful information
  • Making an introduction
  • Setting a later follow-up
  • Determining the relationship is not a fit

Every outcome makes the system smarter.

Step 5: Keep the Relationship Moving

The First Conversation Is Only the Beginning

This is where most referral-development efforts break down. Someone has a good conversation. Then everyone gets busy. Referral Team Pro manages the next step. That can include:

  • Follow-up calls
  • Email check-ins
  • Reminders
  • Meeting follow-up
  • Useful content
  • Customer stories
  • Direct mail
  • Invitations
  • Introductions
  • Future follow-up dates

The relationship stays active because the next action has an owner.

Step 6: Give Partners Something Worth Sharing

Useful Relationships Need Useful Communication

A referral partner should not only hear from you when you want something. We help create reasons to stay connected. That can include:

  • Educational articles
  • Case studies
  • Newsletters
  • Customer stories
  • Videos
  • FAQs
  • Guides
  • Checklists
  • Event invitations
  • Market updates
  • Helpful introductions

Your expertise becomes part of the relationship. When content and authority become a primary strategy, that work can connect directly with MVP Authority Hub .

Authority Hub helps create the expertise. Referral Team Pro helps put it into relationships.

Step 7: Track the Relationship

Your CRM Becomes a Working Relationship System

Referral activity should not live inside inboxes and memory. We document:

  • Contacts
  • Outreach
  • Conversations
  • Meeting history
  • Follow-up dates
  • Relationship stage
  • Introductions
  • Opportunities
  • Referrals
  • Next actions

That gives the team visibility into what is actually happening. It also means the relationship does not disappear when one person gets busy.

Step 8: Measure What Is Developing

Referral Development Can Be Measured

The purpose is not more activity. We want to understand whether relationships are actually developing. Depending on the program, we may track:

  • Qualified Referral Partners: Are we working with the right people?
  • Conversations Started: Is the outreach creating genuine interaction?
  • Meetings: Are relationships moving forward?
  • Active Relationships: How many valuable partnerships are being maintained?
  • Introductions: Are partners beginning to connect us with the right people?
  • Opportunities: Are those introductions becoming real business conversations?
  • Pipeline & Revenue: What business is ultimately being created or influenced?

The exact metrics depend on the program.

Frequently Asked Questions

How involved do I need to be?

We need your expertise and strategic input, especially early in the engagement.

But the point of the program is to remove routine research, outreach, follow-up, CRM work, and relationship management from your desk.

Do I personally have to make the calls?

Not necessarily. Referral Team Pro can manage the initial outreach and routine follow-up.

When a relationship reaches the point where your involvement adds value, we bring you in.

Do we need to change CRMs?

Usually not. Whenever practical, we work inside the systems you already use.

Do you provide the contact data?

Research and contact validation can be part of the program.

Is the outreach automated?

Some elements can be supported by automation, but referral development remains human-led.

We use technology to make the team more efficient, not to replace genuine conversation.

Is there a fixed outreach sequence?

No. The framework is consistent, but the message and channel mix should reflect the audience and relationship.

How long does this take?

Referral development is relationship development. Some opportunities happen quickly. Others develop over time.

We focus on building a repeatable system rather than promising an artificial timetable.

Can Referral Team Pro work with our existing marketing?

Yes. Existing articles, case studies, videos, events, newsletters, and other content can become useful material for maintaining referral relationships.
